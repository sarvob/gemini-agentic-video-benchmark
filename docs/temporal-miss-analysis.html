What agentic video still missed in five frozen tests

Gemini 3.7 Flash with agentic video processing recovered more two-second-or-shorter events overall than the same model in static mode. That advantage did not make temporal understanding uniformly better: one fixture defeated both modes, broad highlight retrieval remained sparse, and agentic boundary errors were larger in the aggregate.

Scope guard. These are five valid pairs of synthetic ten-minute videos. No metric has a two-sided exact sign-flip p-value below 0.05. This analysis does not establish general model superiority, production sports performance, or a Gemini-versus-PaperEdits comparison.

Three different kinds of temporal failure

  • Event omission: agentic missed two of four brief events on podcast-01. Static missed the same count there and one event on each of three other fixtures.
  • Broad-moment under-retrieval: agentic macro moment F1 was 0.2667 versus 0.3000 for static. Agentic led on one fixture, static led on two, and two tied.
  • Boundary and duration error: agentic's average fixture median and p90 errors were 1.2 and 4.0 seconds versus 1.0 and 2.8 seconds for static. On podcast-02, both modes violated the 120-second output limit.

What the public evidence cannot tell us

The committed score records expose counts and aggregate localization errors, but not a public event-by-event match ledger for every run. They therefore support statements such as “two of four brief events were found,” but not a defensible claim about which named event was missed. The evidence also does not isolate whether a miss came from navigation, perception, prompting, schema choices, or selection logic.

How to use this result

For production evaluations, separate brief-event recall, broad-moment retrieval, boundary accuracy, and final edit-duration compliance. Freeze tolerance windows and event-level ground truth before running. Publish the matched ledger when privacy and rights permit, and treat an agentic gain on one temporal layer as a hypothesis—not proof that the whole workflow improved.
